# Lifeora
Lifeora is an AI-powered life companion that combines personal journaling, smart reminders, challenge alarms, habit tracking, daily progress insights, and social networking in one app. Reflect on your day, challenge your mind, connect with people, and grow every day.
# 🌱 Lifeora

### Your Life. Your Growth. Your World.

**Lifeora** is an AI-powered life companion designed to help people organize their daily lives, build positive habits, preserve memories, challenge their minds, and connect with others.

Lifeora brings personal journaling, productivity tools, intelligent insights, interactive challenge alarms, and social networking together in one application.

Our goal is to make everyday life more organized, meaningful, productive, and connected.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📔 Personal Diary
- Write and organize daily diary entries.
- Record memories, activities, thoughts, and feelings.
- Add photos, tags, and mood selections.
- Search previous entries and browse memories by date.
- Protect private entries with app locking.
- Explore past memories with an On This Day feature.

### 🤖 AI Life Companion
- Analyze diary entries with user consent.
- Reflect on daily accomplishments and challenges.
- Compare daily activities and progress.
- Receive personalized suggestions for improvement.
- Generate plans for tomorrow.
- Get weekly and monthly progress summaries.
- Chat with an AI assistant about goals and daily experiences.

### ⏰ Smart Challenge Alarm
- Create alarms with customizable schedules.
- Solve challenges to dismiss an alarm normally.
- Practice mathematics, logic, memory, and general knowledge.
- Explore Kannada riddles, proverbs, and language challenges.
- Choose challenge categories and difficulty levels.
- Track challenge performance and personal records.
- Configure safe emergency dismissal options.

### ✅ Tasks and Daily Planning
- Create tasks, goals, and reminders.
- Organize activities by date, time, and priority.
- Schedule recurring tasks.
- Manage daily plans and upcoming events.
- Receive reminders for important activities.
- View tasks through calendar and list views.

### 🎯 Habits, Missions, and Progress
- Build and track personal habits.
- Set daily and weekly goals.
- Complete daily missions.
- Earn achievements and milestones.
- Monitor task completion and habit consistency.
- Review progress through weekly and monthly reports.

### 🌐 Social Community
- Create a personal profile.
- Share thoughts, statuses, achievements, and photos.
- Discover posts and connect with other users.
- Follow people and manage friendships.
- Like, react to, comment on, and bookmark posts.
- Participate in communities and shared challenges.
- Send private messages.
- Control post visibility and manage privacy settings.

### 📸 Photo Sharing and Memories
- Upload and share photos.
- Add captions and hashtags.
- Organize photos into albums.
- Control who can view shared photos.
- Store supported media in cloud storage.
- Discover public photo collections.
- Manage uploaded content and delete unwanted photos.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Mode
- Organize shared family tasks.
- Manage family reminders and events.
- Coordinate household responsibilities.
- Create shared shopping lists.
- Collaborate with invited family members.

Private diary entries and personal AI conversations remain separate from shared family information.

### 🌍 Multilingual Experience
- English interface.
- Kannada language support.
- Architecture designed for additional Indian languages.

### 🔐 Privacy and Security
- Private diary storage and access controls.
- Optional PIN and biometric app locking.
- Secure authentication.
- Privacy settings for social posts and photos.
- User-controlled AI processing preferences.
- Data export and account deletion.
- Reporting and blocking tools for community safety.

### 💰 Free Access and Optional Premium Features
Lifeora is designed to offer useful core features for free, with carefully placed advertisements and optional premium functionality.

Potential premium features include advanced AI insights, additional challenge packs, enhanced progress reports, and expanded cloud storage.

---

## 🔒 Privacy First

Lifeora is designed around the principle that **personal growth should not require sacrificing privacy**.

Private diary entries, personal reflections, and AI conversations must remain separate from public social content.

Users should control what they share, who can access it, and whether their private entries are processed by AI services.

Sensitive personal content must not be used for advertising personalization.
